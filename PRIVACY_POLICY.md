# Privacy Policy

This mobile application, **FaceAttribute** ("App"), provides users with face recognition, liveness detection and related functions. Protecting the privacy of any personal data processed within the App is important to us.

## Data Collected

- **Face Images and Biometric Templates**: The App uses your device camera to capture face photos and derive biometric templates for recognition and liveness detection. These data are stored only in a local database on your device.
- **Bluetooth and Location Data**: The App requires Bluetooth access to connect to a BLE relay device. Location permission may be requested for Bluetooth scanning to function correctly.
- **Notification Permission**: Notification permission is used solely for delivering notifications related to the App's operation.

## Use of Data

- Face images and templates are processed only within the App for recognition and liveness purposes.
- Collected data is never transmitted to remote servers; it stays entirely on your device.
- The App processes the license key you provide locally for activation. License information is not shared with third parties.

## Data Sharing

No personal data is shared with third parties. All data remains on your device and can be deleted at any time via in-app options.

## Security

The local database is accessible only by the App. Keep your device up to date to prevent unauthorized access.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
